## ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY:

Reconciliation matched Tally and Blinkit invoices, finding rounding matches but substantial unmatched Tally invoices, three amount mismatches and TDS reversals.

## MAIN POINTS:

1. Matching used Tally voucher numbers against Blinkit invoice numbers, comparing debit and amount values.
2. Differences within INR 1.00 were treated as acceptable rounding matches.
3. TDS reversal rows were separated and excluded from invoice matching.
4. Tally contained 58 invoice rows, while Blinkit had 30 bill rows excluding TDS.
5. Blinkit also listed 15 TDS reversal rows totaling INR 157.32.
6. Twenty-six invoices matched within rounding tolerance.
7. Three invoices showed amount mismatches beyond acceptable rounding limits.
8. One likely invoice-number mismatch involved NY/23-24/023 versus NY/23-24/028.
9. Unresolved Tally-only invoices totaled 28 after adjusting for the probable mismatch.
10. Blinkit invoice total excluding TDS was INR 2,324,215.04 versus Tally’s INR 3,449,187.48.

## TAKEAWAYS:

1. Most matched invoices reconcile cleanly, with only minor rounding differences.
2. The major reconciliation issue is missing Blinkit records for numerous Tally invoices.
3. Amount mismatches require review, especially NY/23-24/091 with a INR 35.58 difference.
4. The probable invoice-number mismatch should be verified before treating it as unresolved.
5. TDS reversals should remain separately classified and not offset against invoice matches.
